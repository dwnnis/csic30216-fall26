This course prepares students to work effectively as software engineers in an era of AI-assisted development. The guiding question across all 16 weeks: how does this make you a better software engineer working with AI?

Students develop the ability to specify intent precisely enough for AI agents to execute it, evaluate AI output critically, override AI judgment when necessary, and collaborate on AI-integrated software that other engineers can understand and maintain.
